About · sepia
Moves unique to About:
1. Wordmark + mission is the hero — not a dry heading. One calligraphic glyph in a warm gradient tile, the name, "Read. Reflect. Remember." as a quiet italic.
2. A blessing verse (54:17) in Arabic + translation sits near the top as the emotional anchor — About is where the app can briefly feel reverent.
3. Your stats — verses read, marks, streak — gives the page a personalized feel. Makes returning to About less of a dead end.
4. Install CTA comes early (above versions), since it's the one action a user might actually take here.
5. "With thanks to" framing for attribution — not "Credits" or "Licenses." Tone matters for this kind of app.
6. Honest footer: "Made with care. Not a replacement for a teacher." — appropriate humility for a scripture tool.
