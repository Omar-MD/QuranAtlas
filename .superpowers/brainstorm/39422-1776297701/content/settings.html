"More" menu (from dock ⋯)
Why a bottom sheet, not a page or modal:
• Opens from the ⋯ dock button in one tap — never navigates away from the reader.
• Grip + swipe-down to dismiss (iOS/Android native-feeling). Tap scrim also dismisses.
• Theme picker uses live swatches of the actual theme — you see what you'll get, not just a word.
• Font size slider shows a live preview line in Arabic + English, so you size it for the script that matters to you.
• Translation toggle (on/off) is one tap; tapping the subtitle ("Saheeh International") opens the translation picker as a nested sheet view.
• The "More" menu is the first-level sheet — Settings is one entry inside it. This keeps the dock button truthful ("more things you can do here"), not a Settings shortcut.
