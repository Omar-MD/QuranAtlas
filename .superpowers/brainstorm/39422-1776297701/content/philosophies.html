A · Command-first

A single ⌘K / tap-to-search bar replaces the hamburger, top bar, and bottom nav. Everything — surahs, verses, tags, settings, review — is one keystroke away. Chrome nearly disappears. Linear / Raycast school.

B · Ambient reader

Chrome is invisible while reading. Edge-swipe left → surah picker, right → review. Tap the page → a small pill surfaces (surah · verse · menu). Nav fades back out. Apple Books / Readwise school — the text is the UI.

C · Floating dock

One pill-shaped dock at the bottom: Read · Search · Review · More. No top bar, no hamburger. Tapping Search expands it to a full picker; More opens a bottom sheet for settings. iOS 17 / Arc / Vercel school.

Click the one that feels right — or say "mix A+C", "show me B in dark mode", etc.
