Why these four stats (2×2):
• Marks — the unit of your engagement.
• Tags — thematic breadth (themes you've tracked).
• Surahs (23 / 114) — distributional breadth across the mushaf.
• % of Qur'an tagged (2.3% of 6,236 verses) — verse-level coverage. The "/ 114" and "%" are shown as subtle secondary text inside each stat, so the big number reads first.
Together they describe the shape of your marking without turning it into a compliance game.
