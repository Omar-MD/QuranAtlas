What we take · what we leave

  • Take from quran.com: the semantic drawer (Read / Review / Explore — not just a flat surah list) and the command-bar as search mental model.
  • Take from quranflash: the book feel — chrome gets out of the way when you're reading.
  • Leave behind: tanzil's always-on dense panels, quran.com's persistent top bar + drawer icon, the hamburger.
  • Our differentiator: nobody hides chrome while reading. That's where "ambient reader" actually becomes a product position, not just an aesthetic.
Proposed direction for QuranAtlas — "Ambient + Command":
1. While reading: no top bar, no hamburger, no bottom nav. Just the text.
2. Tap anywhere on the page → a slim pill surfaces at the top ("Al-Fatihah · 1 of 7") and a thin dock at the bottom (Search · Review · More). Auto-fades after 3s.
3. ⌘K / swipe-down / tap the pill → a command sheet opens: surah, verse (2:255), tag (#mercy), action (font size), page (review / settings). One surface replaces the hamburger drawer entirely.
4. Edge-swipe right → review queue. Edge-swipe left → surah picker (quick list, not the full drawer). For one-handed use.
