Design moves unique to the mark editor:
1. Focused verse shows through the scrim — the verse you're marking stays visible behind the sheet with a soft accent outline. You never lose context.
2. Verse quote at top of the sheet as a warm-accent blockquote — same language as notes (gold/bronze left bar). Confirms what you're marking before you act.
3. Tag chips with color dots match the semantic palette used in Review / FVR. Selected chips flip to filled accent; unselected are muted. Count in the label ("Tags · 2 selected").
4. Inline "create" chip with dashed border + plus — appears when your search text doesn't match an existing tag. One tap to mint a new tag.
5. Destructive action is quiet — "⌫ Delete" sits in the left gutter in muted coral. Not a red button. Real destruction happens in the confirm sheet, which requotes the mark + its tags + note so you see exactly what's leaving.
6. Undo toast replaces the dock for 8s after deletion — pill-shaped, same language as the dock, with a clear affordance back.
