4 · Tags intro
Shape. Four screens, progress dots, Skip appears from screen 2 onward (screen 1 has no skip — it's the brand moment). Screen 1 wordmark + 38:29 blessing ("A Book We have sent down to you, blessed"). Screen 2 theme with three swatches (Light / Sepia / Dark) showing the Arabic لفظ الجلالة in each palette + Auto note. Screen 3 translation picker — Saheeh default, offline badge implicit. Screen 4 introduces the core verb: long-press → mark → tag, shown with a real verse (2:286) and sample chips in the semantic palette; ends with privacy line and two CTAs (Fatihah or surah list). Total time < 30 seconds for a user who reads everything.
