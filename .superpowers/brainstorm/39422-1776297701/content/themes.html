Notes on the palettes:
• Dark uses warm off-black (#0e0e0c) rather than pure black — softer on OLED, less clinical. Gold accent (#a89968) reads as "illuminated manuscript" rather than "app UI".
• Sepia is intentionally warmer than most reader apps — inspired by aged Qur'an parchment. Bronze accent keeps it cohesive with the gold from dark mode (same hue family).
• All three themes (light from earlier, dark, sepia) share the same gold/bronze accent family → theme switching feels like changing paper, not changing apps.
